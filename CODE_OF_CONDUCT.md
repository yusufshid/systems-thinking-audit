# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, be constructive, and assume good faith.

- Disagree with ideas, not people. A critique of a checklist item or a finding in a report is welcome; personal attacks aren't.
- Keep discussion focused on the project — the skill's framework, its evals, its documentation.
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in a ban from the repo.
- If someone's behavior is a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer directly rather than escalating in public threads.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/); a full formal version can be adopted later if the project's contributor base grows enough to need it.
